## Desk disagreements and decisive independent findings

- T20 loses its “likely-pass” design status here despite the strong accepted shape: the proposed reference is an ordinary full harness, and permitted calculations followed by actor echo satisfy response receipts without proving actor derivation. T02 also drops from “likely-pass” to FIX pending an actual residual and raw-loss/retention feasibility.
- T01/T12 use “edge”, “thin” or “region” labels for weighted RGB error. Masks change weights, not the underlying semantic quantity. Midpoint/blur and vector-mosaic controls threaten native headroom before any learner experiment.
- T06 has an algebraic allocation counterexample: at fixed average per-attempt success, concentrating certainty on easy tasks can increase the A/C/T mean while reducing coverage. Its fixed denominator and fresh-seed defenses do not solve this.
- T10’s itemwise feasible observation path can be selected using hidden truth. It does not prove that one nonanticipatory pixels-only policy can find the right evidence within budget. Similar identifiability risks affect the T13 bridge, T15 compatible worlds and T16 user laws.
- T22 has unresolved full native loss atomization, teacher-call economics and feature/kernel semantics. A bounded positive-definite kernel alone is not a semantic discriminator. T23 can reward simple trajectory outputs; prohibiting a procedural renderer at deployment does not block legal neural imitation.
- Task-specific raw per-slice floors are absent or deferred despite repeated B14 wording. Fixed denominators stop missing-row laundering; they do not prevent profitable legal specialization. Every attack table treats lawful specialization separately from invalid omission.
- Source/AST caps, nonce text and root-owned path names are specification controls, not measured barriers. A procedural solver can be legal, a small operator can synthesize forbidden state, and an authenticated model response can echo a precomputed answer. Typed causal dataflow and a competent recipe floor must both be tested.
