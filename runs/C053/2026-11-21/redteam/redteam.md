## Published recipe shortcut — web checked

- [Identification, Doubly Robust Estimation, and Semiparametric Efficiency Theory of Nonignorable Missing Data With a Shadow Variable](https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.02556) provides explicit conditions under which informative missingness can be identified using auxiliary evidence. A proxy merely being correlated is not sufficient to assert those conditions here.
- [Nonparametric inference about mean functionals of nonignorable nonresponse data without identifying the joint distribution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.05776) establishes that a decision-relevant mean can be identifiable even when the full distribution is not. Applying that insight to recipe ranking is an inference, not a theorem about this task.
- [Bayesian Experimental Design: A Review](https://www.stat.cmu.edu/tr/tr599/tr599.html) frames experiment choice as expected-utility maximization, the standard basis for deciding which absent reports merit replication.

Each primary abstract page was opened in this session. Verification did not extend to proofs, estimator assumptions on the unpublished generator, or runnable implementations. ## Revised estimate versus the source card

The card's source ESTIMATED 0.49 already considers multiple retrieval routes, selection modeling and replication but discounts absences with identical visible traces. My ESTIMATED 0.73 composite does not require each cause to be individually recognized. It can integrate over ambiguous causes, learn only the value-relevant functional and replicate when that uncertainty changes the optimal recipe. Shared visible traces reduce the information available for state attribution; they do not necessarily make the actual decision difficult. Conversely, if those traces hide different optimal follow-ups with no informative probe, the environment needs a Bayesian achievable bound rather than a clairvoyant answer key.

This is not an argument that generic inverse-probability weighting solves arbitrary informative missingness. Such methods require assumptions, valid auxiliary information and suitable support. The point is that the source never specifies the mechanism tightly enough to assess those requirements, while its designer estimate treats uncertainty about identification as evidence of difficulty. The revised version must prove a useful legal observation pathway and then challenge the strongest compatible estimator/acquisition controller within it.
