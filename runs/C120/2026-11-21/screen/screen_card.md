## 2. Strongest lawful combined attack
Represent artifacts, executions and permitted derivations as an immutable dependency graph, with an explicit authorization rule on each transformation. [ML Metadata](https://www.tensorflow.org/tfx/guide/mlmd?authuser=90) verifies an existing artifact/execution lineage data model; it records provenance but does not itself enforce noninterference. [Datalog as a Lingua Franca for Provenance Querying and Reasoning](https://www.usenix.org/conference/tapp12/workshop-program/presentation/dey) verifies an established way to express provenance integrity constraints. Combine these with a trusted admission service and separate protected evaluation processes.

The attacker would compute transitive ancestry, preserve legal source identities through allowed transformations, and reject any path that imports protected outcomes. Avoid blanket tainting by distinguishing lawful metadata, legal source text and forbidden labels in the declared schema. Generate fresh legal examples from admitted sources, verify them independently and train the agent using standard checked self-training. On the finite transformation interface, enumerate combinations to recover every productive authorized path rather than rely on textual reminders. Keep the policy outside candidate-controlled emitters, log attempted admissions, and pay for legal data construction and training. This is a competent dataflow compiler plus a learning recipe, which is exactly the stated intervention.

## 3. Other routes
A strict typed pipeline with a conservative legal transformation whitelist is the strongest single standard recipe. ESTIMATED R=0.73, range 0.51–0.90. It may discard some useful paths but should already eliminate accidental contamination while supporting most ordinary training.

Enumerating authorized transformation chains and sweeping admission granularity has ESTIMATED R=0.84, range 0.64–0.95. CPU closure analysis can identify which withheld paths are provably lawful before expensive training. A lawful shortcut rebuilds the training corpus directly from clean sources and ignores exploratory outputs entirely: ESTIMATED R=0.78, range 0.49–0.94. If sufficient clean data exists, that route avoids the claimed ambiguity and must be admitted as a real baseline.

That would be a substantially different acquisition task, not a small repair to this scaffold card.
