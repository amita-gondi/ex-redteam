## Task 08A

**Design proxy experiments that transfer a continued-pretraining policy across scale and horizon — VERDICT: cut.**

Current raw formula: `S = mean_v [0.50 exp(−l_v) + 0.50 a_v].` See [08A §Reward](/workspace/task_design_v2_codex/08_experimental_design_and_transfer.md:127). All table payouts are estimates using F above; actual anchors and attainable outcomes are to verify.

### Audit of the task’s own saturation section and kill criteria

Its own section already labels this the highest-risk metric-feasibility candidate and says to cut if native differences cannot support the gate. That is the correct disposition until evidence exists. Zero entropy is not a feasible ceiling; bigger horizon/history diversity does not defeat a universal target-feedback controller. Do not spend the stated full validation budget to discover the exp(−loss) compression issue. Re-entry requires a metric redesign and an experimentally necessary transfer decision. Source: [08A §Saturation resistance](/workspace/task_design_v2_codex/08_experimental_design_and_transfer.md:149); experiments: [08A §E2/E3](/workspace/task_design_v2_codex/08_experimental_design_and_transfer.md:190). The common protocol critique above also applies.

### Verdict justification

Cut this version from the build queue. The loss transform can make the central continued-pretraining outcome nearly dead, while the capability component carries the raw-headroom requirement. A broad deployed controller can also perform direct target search instead of learning to transfer experiments. These are structural design issues, not just missing measurements; rescue would require a new metric/decision contract and fresh feasibility evidence.
