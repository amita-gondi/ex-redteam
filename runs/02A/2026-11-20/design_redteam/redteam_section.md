## Task 02A

**Create transferable learning signal beyond a policy’s success frontier — VERDICT: needs fixes.**

Current raw formula: `S = 0.70 a + 0.30 r, with hint-free exact success on original and perturbed tasks.` See [02A §Reward](/workspace/task_design_v2_codex/02_objectives_and_credit.md:127). All table payouts are estimates using F above; actual anchors and attainable outcomes are to verify.

### Audit of the task’s own saturation section and kill criteria

The section correctly names annealed hints as a fatal shortcut, but a generic mention is not a faithful rationalization baseline. Perturbed copies do not increase the number of independent skills. Its depth and aliasing knobs risk making the 8B learner unlearnable rather than requiring more research from a 10T solver. Use oracle-demonstration feasibility and full-budget STaR controls before applying the shared maximum/best-of-k gates. Source: [02A §Saturation resistance](/workspace/task_design_v2_codex/02_objectives_and_credit.md:149); experiments: [02A §E2/E3](/workspace/task_design_v2_codex/02_objectives_and_credit.md:190). The common protocol critique above also applies.

### Verdict justification

The task measures final hint-free behavior and has a plausible RSI role. Its allowed privileged supervision creates direct supervised routes around absent terminal rewards, and the common loss-plugin boundary is not yet concrete. It needs stronger named baselines, demonstrated dependency edges and a structurally enforceable training interface. Merge with 01B if only curriculum/acquisition differentiates their successful methods.
