## Task 04B

**Co-design precision and correction for a learner whose activation distribution drifts — VERDICT: needs fixes.**

Current raw formula: `S = 0.80 a + 0.20 e, with semantically valid useful-token throughput e.` See [04B §Reward](/workspace/task_design_v2_codex/04_training_fidelity.md:259). All table payouts are estimates using F above; actual anchors and attainable outcomes are to verify.

### Audit of the task’s own saturation section and kill criteria

The preset-sweep warning is well chosen, but the task could fail before saturation measurement: equal-quality numerical efficiency has less than 0.20 raw possible gain. Increasing drift can only be legitimate if a healthy baseline experiences the same real regime, not if the author manufactures divergence. Current scaling and a generic error-budget policy must precede S1–S4. The formal FP8 peak is not attainable-frontier evidence. Source: [04B §Saturation resistance](/workspace/task_design_v2_codex/04_training_fidelity.md:281); experiments: [04B §E2/E3](/workspace/task_design_v2_codex/04_training_fidelity.md:322). The common protocol critique above also applies.

### Verdict justification

Retain the numerical-fidelity question only after reconciling its metric with the physical-headroom gate. An honestly healthy bf16 floor leaves insufficient raw room for a pure speed improvement, while current scaling and selective fallback may already prevent the drifting error. The design needs a concrete frontier, stronger complete defaults and probe/rare-slice hardening before its depth can be assessed.
