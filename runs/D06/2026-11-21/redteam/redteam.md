## Saturation against the hypothetical 10T researcher / 12h

Unlike a generic fixed-price planning task, D06 could retain a real learning obstacle if repairing support changes what subsequent examples teach. That requires observation-matched evidence of a bridge, enough budget to investigate it, and genuine loss when replacing it. Calling a world unique by metadata is insufficient: the solver may generate an equivalent prerequisite through the lawful factory. Likewise, hiding support identity until an irreversible decision without any predictive probe would make the task luck-driven rather than research-driven.

A strong researcher can combine repair diagnosis, coverage planning and trained response prediction. Its planning overhead may be modest relative to real learner continuations, so the important limit is paid learner evidence. The reference must not receive extra causal probes or a precomputed utility table. Whole-portfolio search can bypass the stated sequence of insights and remains valid. The current weakness reports' early-stopping and weak-verification examples do not establish resistance to such a complete researcher.

## Verdict, required fixes and cheapest decisive check

FIX requires:

1. Construct actual campaigns where unique recoverable prerequisite support changes later learning, with a common causal observation/probe policy and no hidden future-return table.
2. Measure clean replacement, fix-all plus portfolio selection, and interaction-aware lookahead under the same irreversible budget. Demonstrate a native reference advantage; if support can be synthesized cheaply or the composite matches it, cut.
3. Implement root-owned persistent debits for construction, failures, resets, retained caches and discarded worlds; bind every trained inventory to validated immutable handles. Keep shared sunk assets consistent across methods.
4. Freeze broad/vulnerable cohorts, local capability floors, matched diagnostic proxies, calibrated attainable controls and complete fault/timeout semantics. Ship portable isolated workers and the Round 2 live exploit/independent positive regression suite.

Cheapest decisive experiment: paired repair-only, clean-replacement, interaction-aware portfolio and proposed adaptive-controller campaigns, with real learner training and fixed terminal evaluation. **ESTIMATED 14.4 H100-hours** comprises **ESTIMATED 4 arms × 3 paired seeds × 0.95 H100-hours** for learner work and **ESTIMATED 3 H100-hours** for paid diagnostic learner probes and endpoint evaluation. It is a headroom kill pilot, not a full saturation study or spend authorization.
