## Task 03B

**Learn a reasoning policy that allocates variable inference budgets without losing coverage — VERDICT: needs fixes.**

Current raw formula: `S = 0.60 a + 0.30 h + 0.10 z; h is hard-tier pass@8 and z=1−mean((p−y)^2).` See [03B §Reward](/workspace/task_design_v2_codex/03_knowledge_transfer.md:259). All table payouts are estimates using F above; actual anchors and attainable outcomes are to verify.

### Audit of the task’s own saturation section and kill criteria

The section accurately warns that shortest-correct data may dominate, but the more basic issue is that an actual-cost optimum is absent from the reward. Declared budgets provide a real resource constraint, not an incentive for economical allocation within it. Its perfect calibration/coverage corner can be unattainable at the lowest cap. Fix the objective and h estimand before using S1–S4; then test the combined shortest-correct/GKD baseline and full-cap policy against the revised frontier. Source: [03B §Saturation resistance](/workspace/task_design_v2_codex/03_knowledge_transfer.md:281); experiments: [03B §E2/E3](/workspace/task_design_v2_codex/03_knowledge_transfer.md:322). The common protocol critique above also applies.

### Verdict justification

Retain a repaired budgeted-reasoning task, but the current score does not support the claim that it learns efficient per-request allocation. Always using the full cap is rational and can win; pass@8 and the Brier term add their own distributional incentives. Define whether the desired outcome is capped capability or actual efficiency, specify hard-tier aggregation, and then validate a substantial gap over the full combined standard recipe.

## Primary sources used for attack recipes

- **[P6](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.13649)** [On-Policy Distillation of Language Models: Learning from Self-Generated Mistakes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.13649). The cited on-policy/generalized distillation baseline. Multi-teacher executable filtering here is a proposed attack composition.
- **[P17](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02629)** [Generalization in Adaptive Data Analysis and Holdout Reuse](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02629). Motivates controlling adaptive holdout reuse. A throttle alone is not a privacy or generalization proof; no complete v2 bank reconstruction is claimed.
