## 6. Redesign
Cause (a): mature source recovery solves much of the stated pager failure. Provide a source-recoverable ledger and folding runtime to all contenders. Submit a decision-aware retrieval scheduler over authentic campaigns where later observations change the relevant earlier contrast, such as distinguishing data-quality effects from optimizer instability after a resumed checkpoint. Match development conditions, disclose dependencies that a researcher can legitimately observe, and charge real retrieval/inference costs; avoid padded logs or arbitrary context starvation.
