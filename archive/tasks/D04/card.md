## Why known recipes plateau

Model checking can certify transition invariants but does not establish instructional value. Constraint sampling, exhaustive valid-world enumeration, diversity selection and ordinary competence curricula form the complete baseline. The proposed residual is finding support whose usefulness becomes visible only after earlier training changes the learner's failure modes; no plateau has been measured.

## Deterministic grader

Execute generated worlds under trusted validity and reachability checks, then train the fixed learner on the admitted curriculum. U is exact terminal repair success on an independently authored target collection, macro-averaged by mechanism. Invalid generated examples are excluded from training while consuming their declared generation budget; target failures remain scored. Factory validity earns no standalone reward. Map U through F_D04.

## Hidden-score design

Target mechanisms are written independently of the factory grammar's surface forms. Dev includes a protected target-family proxy and identical admission/training limits. The factory cannot emit scorer code, private targets or arbitrary trainer mutations. Histories must come from actual inherited or author pilot runs, not fabricated logs.
