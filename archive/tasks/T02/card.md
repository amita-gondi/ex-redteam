## Deterministic grader and exact reward contract

The trusted scorer constructs every evaluation target directly from immutable raw document bytes. For private replay seed j and mandatory slice s, let N_s be the committed positive number of eligible targets and let `L[j,s] = sum_{d,t in s} -log p_theta_j(x[d,t] | fixed_context[d,t]) / N_s`. Missing documents or failed forward calls do not reduce N_s. Any unauthorized mutation or malformed recipe is an invalid submission. A candidate-induced replay crash, OOM, nonfinite trained state, missing state or timeout gives zero utility for that replay seed; other independent seed grades remain. A rare ordinary evaluation-item failure loses that document's committed utility share, rather than dropping its tokens. Exact per-document lost shares are recorded. Infrastructure faults are different typed parent-adjudicated statuses and remain pending HOLD until resolved.

Define a training-only unigram model over the entire fixed vocabulary V: `p_uni(v) = (count_train(v)+1)/(sum_u count_train(u)+|V|)`. Counts use the immutable permitted pool before any solver exposure, including EOS and excluding BOS/padding. Its per-slice evaluation code length is `H[s] = sum_{d,t in s} -log p_uni(x[d,t])/N_s`, under the identical target denominator. H is fixed by the split and never estimated from the candidate. It is not the entropy rate of natural language. The frozen manifest must contain its numeric values and reproducible counts, tokenizer hash and reduction version. A nonpositive H or empty slice blocks freeze.

The proposed utility is deliberately not divided by a tiny reference gain:

`q[j,s] = clip(1 - L[j,s]/H[s], 0, 1)`.

For each slice, let `q_base[s]` be the competent corrected starter's independently calibrated seed mean of `clip(1-L_base[j,s]/H_calibration[s],0,1)`, before applying any floor penalty.
