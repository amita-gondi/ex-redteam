## Problem and research value

A multimodal post-training researcher inherits a VLM that answers ordinary questions plausibly while failing matched examples in which changing the image or reversing an event changes the answer. The researcher must improve its learned, unprivileged behavior on static relations, event order, visible localization and complete event timelines. Training annotations may include masks and event identity. Deployment receives pixels, the question and a public answer grammar; it receives no scene program, object-state table, mask hint, privileged crop or hidden event annotation.

The important lab decision is where to spend training effort after ordinary counterfactual augmentation: obtain missing joint examples, improve the evidence-to-answer learning signal, preserve rare temporal behavior during mixed training, or change how training privilege is withdrawn. Better language priors alone should fail matched truth-changing media pairs. Mere sensitivity to an image is insufficient: becoming consistently wrong when the image changes earns nothing. The outcome is correct grounded behavior, not a report claiming grounding.

This is closest in shape to GDM's accepted **pretraining_latest**: a working model and training service, multi-hour experiments, bounded training modifications and an independently executed learned-outcome grade. Like **inference_optimization**, actual accelerator work must be captured and checked, although T11 rewards capability rather than throughput. Like **agentic_search_inference_efficiency**, development feedback must predict unseen content; T11 avoids its hosted-answer variability by evaluating the subject locally. These comparisons establish a proposed workflow, not accepted status for T11. If the whole gain comes from reading a paper or enumerating augmentation settings, the task fails its research premise.

T11 trains an actor. A learned grader or evidence evaluator is outside its scope. Model souping, checkpoint averaging, model merging, specialist fusion and external teacher-model downloads are excluded. Selecting one independently trained branch is allowed; every branch is charged.

The following definitions are the exact proposed scorer; **all newly chosen numeric coefficients in this subsection are ESTIMATED design constants**:

* Static relation and temporal-order member utility is the indicator of the exact ontology answer. Order answers refer to the displayed event IDs, not guessed hidden event programs. For a matched pair, utility is the minimum of its member utilities: both factual answers must be correct. The pair is the declared independent reward unit. * Localization member utility is visible-mask IoU, `|P∩G|/|P∪G|`, on the frozen decoded grid. A run-length mask is bounded to the grid size. No amodal mask or box is substituted. * A timeline prediction is a list of `(event_label, role_tuple, start, end)` over integer half-open intervals `[start,end)` in displayed frame units. Require `0≤start<end≤T`; duplicates are retained and penalized, not silently deduplicated. Maximum list length is **ESTIMATED 64**, the same cap used to bound all gold banks. Golds are maximal contiguous visible event occurrences with public onset/offset rules. No hidden temporal tolerance exists.
* For gold interval g and prediction p with the same label and role tuple, `tIoU(g,p)=max(0,min(g.end,p.end)−max(g.start,p.start))/(max(g.end,p.end)−min(g.start,p.start))`; otherwise edge weight is zero. Select a one-to-one maximum-weight bipartite matching M. Member utility is `sum_(g,p in M) tIoU(g,p)/max(|G|,|P|)`. Both lists empty gives one only on predeclared truly empty episodes; just one empty gives zero. Matching uses exact rational edge weights with deterministic lexicographic tie resolution. Pair utility is the minimum member utility. No threshold step converts a near-miss interval into a total failure.

### Strongest remaining attack estimate

The strongest remaining **lawful** attack is to learn a reusable object/event parser by direct training labels, exhaust all affordable joint interventions and then optimize the terminal answer/timeline policy with ordinary balanced SFT/GRPO plus CFPO-style consistency. The causal renderer grammar may be small enough that new compositions add little. A stronger researcher can combine this with training-only program synthesis, schedule search and dev selection without following the proposed chain.

My working stress forecast is **ESTIMATED R≈0.58, with ESTIMATED scenario range 0.35–0.84**, agreeing with the independent review's caution rather than the desk review's **source-file ESTIMATED 0.38**. These are not identifiable calibrated predictions. To make the point numerically checkable under the new map, a hypothetical **ESTIMATED (b,c,d)=(0.30,0.55,0.75)** and **ESTIMATED U_attack≈0.5417** give **ESTIMATED R≈0.58**; the range corresponds to **ESTIMATED U≈0.4458–0.67**. None of these U/anchor values was measured or selected for release. A near-reference lawful parser would almost erase the required frontier margin; the upper scenario would trigger an absolute kill. The argument is the concrete available training route and small-program closure, not an average of guessed slice gains.

If this exact fixed recipe is already represented competently in calibration b, its calibration reward is zero by design. The stress estimate concerns a stronger searched version or a transfer reversal on fresh families; it cannot be cited as an expected positive reward for a method simultaneously assumed to define the floor on the same content. Report `F(U_actual)` after calibration and reject this forecast if evidence disagrees. Security fixes provide no reason to lower a lawful method's raw utility.
