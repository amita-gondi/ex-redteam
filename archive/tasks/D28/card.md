## Why known recipes plateau

Strong behavioral cloning, privileged-teacher distillation, version-corrected RL and counterfactual action feedback are complete baselines. The possible residual is that transition-grounded credit changes the learner's future visual failures, which then changes useful supervision. That interaction is unproved, and teacher demonstrations may supply the answer directly.

## Deterministic grader

Trusted workers bind delayed transition outcomes to original policy/action versions and rebuild the learner using submitted feedback rules. Deploy without DOM access on private screenshot workflows. U is exact durable terminal success across asynchronous and ordinary tasks, including failed/late episodes. Local token accuracy and teacher agreement do not enter F_D28.

Explicitly test ambiguous screenshot states for legal history-based identifiability; no impossible hidden distinction is rewarded.
