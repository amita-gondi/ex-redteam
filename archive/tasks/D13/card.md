**Generator:** evidence-obligation recovery. **Stage × artifact:** research process × model weights. **Modality:** ML incident probes and certificates.

## Problem

An incident investigator can restore an ML pipeline by guessing a fix while omitting evidence that distinguishes competing causes. Train an investigator that buys probes, repairs the incident and emits a machine-checkable causal certificate. [Suite 5.13](/workspace/task_suites_claude.md:6537) and Suite 9.9 require explicit claim obligations, so strategic silence cannot receive perfect evidential credit.

## Why known recipes plateau

For bounded recoverable fault graphs, active diagnosis, hitting-set planning and synthetic demonstration generation appear sufficient. A more authentic ML mechanism might create a residual, but no such instance is established. Enlarging a Boolean fault graph would make the same planner slower without changing the insight.

## Deterministic grader

Root executes repairs against exact task outcomes and checks certificates against logged paid interventions. U combines repaired terminal capability with complete support for predeclared causal obligations, using frozen component weights. Missing claims lose their assigned obligation credit; unsupported claims fail exact checks. Alternative valid certificates must be accepted. F_D13 cannot reward eloquent explanations or an empty report.

## Hidden-score design

Hidden fault graphs and replay keys remain outside the investigator, including during training-data generation.
