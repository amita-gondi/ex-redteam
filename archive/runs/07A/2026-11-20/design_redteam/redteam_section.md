## Task 07A

**Repair an RL measurement ecosystem and prove that optimization learns the intended skill — VERDICT: needs fixes.**

### Audit of the task’s own saturation section and kill criteria

The own-section universal-hardening cut is credible but should run before costly inner training. A fixed bank plus finite-budget attacker does not have an inherently distant ceiling, and adding independent holes is not a research chain. The reward needs a downstream-learning guard and timeout/attacker-health semantics before applying the shared maxima/best-of-k kills. Treat adversarially correct artifacts as preservation tests, not attacks to reject. Source: [07A §Saturation resistance](/workspace/task_design_v2_codex/07_measurement_and_reward.md:149); experiments: [07A §E2/E3](/workspace/task_design_v2_codex/07_measurement_and_reward.md:190). The common protocol critique above also applies.

### Verdict justification

Keep the idea as a conditional integrity task, but correct the reward logic and adaptive-attacker interface. A solver can collect most checker credit without improving the learner, or make adaptive attacks ineffective by obstructing them. The outer executable truth architecture is good; it needs tests showing that a universal checker baseline leaves a meaningful semantic/learning problem and that attacker failure cannot count as successful defense.
