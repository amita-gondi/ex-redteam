## 6. Redesign
Class b; REDESIGNABLE. Publish the terminal horizon, proper scoring rule, utility tradeoff and complete probe ledger before agent evaluation. Include real data-repetition mechanisms where a small targeted intervention predicts a later regularizer reversal, and show those mechanisms on matched development corpora. A reference must exploit early evidence rather than unavailable information.
