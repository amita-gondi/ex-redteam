# Independent review method

This review was commissioned separately from the catalog authoring session. I did not design the catalog. I formed mechanism/metric/shortcut objections from the packets, binding bar, feedback and primary web sources before comparing the desk's numerical scenarios. This is a CPU document and source audit, not a live container attack, benchmark, training run, security certification or measured saturation result. No subject-model experiments are authorized or performed.

## Numerical and verdict semantics

Every new performance number, uncertainty range, fraction, cost, time allocation and derived quantity in this review is **ESTIMATED**, including equations and table cells, unless explicitly identified as a source-file observation or binding threshold. IDs and citation years are identifiers. File counts/hashes/word counts from the validation script are observed document properties, not task results. Conditional post-fix `ESTIMATED 0` means the required negative-PoC outcome if the specified boundary is correctly implemented; it is never a test result. A legal strategy is never invalidated merely for bypassing the intended method.

The reward endpoints b/u, instances and executable references are unresolved in these packets. Therefore no calibrated numerical strongest-attack prediction is identifiable. My working reward estimates are stress scenarios conditional on the proposed reference being placed around the packet's stated midrange and its stronger legal control remaining reachable. Their broad ranges are subjective scenario ranges, not confidence intervals. Actual identified reward bounds remain the entire permitted range until anchors exist. Neither my estimates nor the desk's support a statistical PASS or an observed S2 kill. Verdicts are independent design decisions: **HOLDS** = plausible as currently specified subject to mandatory empirical gates; **FIX** = a specific bounded redesign could retain a credible unsolved mechanism; **CUT** = current task lacks a plausible 10T/12h resistance case, has a structural metric/recipe collapse, or lacks a defensible essential outcome. A different future distribution can be re-entered; forbidding a legal winning method cannot rescue it.

For every task the strongest attack includes both dishonest channels and lawful complete algorithms. Tables distinguish a defense that blocks a route *in the specification* from implementation evidence. No packet's proposed filenames establish that a sandbox, reference, test bank or query service exists. Current exploit payoffs are conditional on the specified vulnerability; they are not claims that a vulnerable built implementation was found.

## Binding gate used for every report

From ACCEPTANCE_BAR_GENERAL_v2.md S1–S7: reference R in [0.50,0.65], feasible same-deployment stronger control at least reference+0.20 and reachable R≥0.75; immediate observed kill for any valid full-budget run>0.70, oracle best-of-4 mean>0.60, or pass@4>0.50 at R≥0.60. Projected upper-95%≥0.75 kills; unidentifiable extrapolation or intervals touching limits remain HOLD. Apply the additional ceiling-relative Q gate with an independently measured feasible ceiling lower bound. A physically low maximum does not rescue a task. A10/B12 require a competent combined recipe floor and genuine native reserve before reward scaling. S3 separates researcher tokens, serial time, parallel resources and subject compute. S5 needs content-fresh coupled mechanisms and feasible harder settings, not secret seeds or more examples. S6 reports oracle and lawful dev selection and charges every attempt.

The common future saturation test is the full source-bar sweep and independently authored instance blocks, followed by best-of-k/pass@k and conditional stronger-tier/compute projections. No per-task shortcut paragraph waives any of S1–S7. Pilots in the reports can kill or expose nonidentifiability; they cannot certify resistance. The 10T/12h target is the user's hypothetical researcher, not an asserted specification of any public model.

## Use of weakness reports

Both supplied weakness reports were read as evidence inventories, including their caveats. Reports of poor iteration, early stopping, weak verification, narrow exploration and incremental research motivate learning/control hypotheses. They do not predict a larger researcher's ceiling. The same reports explicitly describe strength on familiar recipes, possible harness artifacts, older-model comparisons, vendor evidence and unverified/secondhand numbers. I do not promote those performance values into independently verified facts. In particular, I assume forced full-budget search, reliable note retention, executable checks and complete paper recall when attacking; tasks depending on current agent passivity are cut. No external numeric model comparison from those reports is relied on here.

## Boilerplate accounting

`evidence/document_audit.json` records observed word counts and a reproducible *section allocation*: acceptance-status tables, numeric-bar restatements, item-by-item feedback crosswalk, generic results shell and common process text. That allocation is a conservative indicator, not a plagiarism detector. Per-report generic-text fractions are ESTIMATED editorial ranges after accounting for repeated task nouns inside templates. “Mostly boilerplate” means build-critical semantics are still missing; it is not a criticism of repeating necessary security requirements. The distinctive problem, ABI, metric, reference and source contributions are reviewed separately.

## Scope and reproducibility

Only this output tree is writable for this task. `evidence/source_manifest.json` records hashes of reviewed inputs. `evidence/web_*.json` preserves web search/read tool returns. Primary sources establish that an algorithm/tool exists and what mechanism it addresses, not its score on an unbuilt task. Verification depth is stated in each report. Search failure is not proof of novelty; version/ABI/rights verification is separate from web discovery. Structural text/JSON/link/count checks are the only executed checks. Proposed GPU-h in reports and budgets are contingent estimates; spent/authorized experiment GPU-h are zero (user prohibition).
