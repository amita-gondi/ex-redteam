## Task 02B

**Train a research agent to assign credit across delayed and irreversible tool actions — VERDICT: needs fixes.**

Current raw formula: `S = 0.60 a + 0.40 q, where q is the fraction of graphs solved on all eight attempts.` See [02B §Reward](/workspace/task_design_v2_codex/02_objectives_and_credit.md:259). All table payouts are estimates using F above; actual anchors and attainable outcomes are to verify.

### Audit of the task’s own saturation section and kill criteria

The own-section planner-SFT cut criterion is the right one but untested. Longer horizons do not prevent canonical-state tracking and can amplify the convex q specialization incentive. The bounded memory channel is an additional deployed solver unless explicitly constrained. Kill or redesign if a planner in memory or planner-generated SFT reaches the reference; apply the shared best-of-k rules only after those baselines and the reliability estimand are fixed. Source: [02B §Saturation resistance](/workspace/task_design_v2_codex/02_objectives_and_credit.md:281); experiments: [02B §E2/E3](/workspace/task_design_v2_codex/02_objectives_and_credit.md:322). The common protocol critique above also applies.

### Verdict justification

The artifact ancestry setting could teach useful long-horizon learning, but its most important boundaries are unspecified. Executable memory may solve the task outside the policy, and q can reward abandoning hard graphs at unchanged mean success. Repair those two issues and establish a gap over strong planner SFT before retaining a full-budget credit-assignment task.

## Primary sources used for attack recipes

- **[P4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.14465)** [STaR: Bootstrapping Reasoning With Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.14465). Rationalization using correct answers followed by training on successful rationales; transfer to the proposed private compositions is to verify.
- **[P5](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.07857)** [RUDDER: Return Decomposition for Delayed Rewards](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.07857). Generic return redistribution for delayed rewards; applying it to an 8B workflow policy is an untested adaptation.
- **[P17](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02629)** [Generalization in Adaptive Data Analysis and Holdout Reuse](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02629). Motivates controlling adaptive holdout reuse. A throttle alone is not a privacy or generalization proof; no complete v2 bank reconstruction is claimed.
