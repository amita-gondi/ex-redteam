## 6. Redesign
Class (a), mature memory/retrieval machinery. REDESIGNABLE. Use authentic continuation-project sequences where the same optimizer mechanism remains useful but the best numerical prescription changes with learnable data/state conditions. Expose full authorized histories, including negative and conflicting experiments. A cheap local probe should sometimes rank recipes differently from meaningful later training, with representative dev examples of that reversal. Revalidation costs follow actual learning-curve duration, not an artificial access fee.

Score final private learning gains and retained useful reuse after total retrieval, context and experiment costs. Include no memory, observation-only retrieval, Zep/Mem0-style updates and probe-gated recommendation reuse in the joint floor. The strongest re-attack adds MemEvolve-style policy search and a value-of-information selector. It cannot resolve every applicability question by checking a version tag or repeating a universally predictive miniature experiment; it must decide which historical evidence transfers across a real learning transition. This is valuable research process automation. Risk: robust generic recipes make historical distinctions irrelevant, in which case the task should be cut rather than banning them.
