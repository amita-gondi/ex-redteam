## Task 08B

**Co-design midtraining for the downstream learning it enables — VERDICT: cut.**

Current raw formula: `S = 0.75 a_after + 0.25 r_retained, averaged across downstream variants.` See [08B §Reward](/workspace/task_design_v2_codex/08_experimental_design_and_transfer.md:259). All table payouts are estimates using F above; actual anchors and attainable outcomes are to verify.

### Audit of the task’s own saturation section and kill criteria

The own section explicitly offers merging into 01A when stage effects are small. Even large final gains do not prove the intended trainability mechanism: pre-adaptation scores are diagnostic only. More adaptation regimes can be solved by the same conservative replay policy or make proxy ranking irreducibly unreliable. Use zero-stage and direct-capability controls first; a separate costly S1–S4 campaign is unwarranted without a distinct causal estimand. Source: [08B §Saturation resistance](/workspace/task_design_v2_codex/08_experimental_design_and_transfer.md:281); experiments: [08B §E2/E3](/workspace/task_design_v2_codex/08_experimental_design_and_transfer.md:322). The common protocol critique above also applies.

### Verdict justification

Cut this standalone task and merge its downstream-adaptation evaluation into 01A. The current reward cannot distinguish a better starting model from a model that learns better, and the allowed stage/controller space overlaps data/stage schedule research. This is a design-level scope recommendation, not a measured failure. Re-entry needs a distinct causal trainability objective, endpoint controls and proven predictable proxy transfer.

## Primary sources used for attack recipes

- **[P2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.06560)** [Hyperband: A Novel Bandit-Based Approach to Hyperparameter Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.06560). Successive-halving resource allocation; no published result is claimed on these proposed tasks.
- **[P15](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.03466)** [Tensor Programs V: Tuning Large Neural Networks via Zero-Shot Hyperparameter Transfer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.03466). A concrete transfer baseline when parameterization permits it; a history-dependent continuation controller is a proposed extension.
- **[P16](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.09846)** [Population Based Training of Neural Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.09846). A generic online schedule-search baseline, not a published solution to the proposed target states.
- **[P17](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02629)** [Generalization in Adaptive Data Analysis and Holdout Reuse](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02629). Motivates controlling adaptive holdout reuse. A throttle alone is not a privacy or generalization proof; no complete v2 bank reconstruction is claimed.
