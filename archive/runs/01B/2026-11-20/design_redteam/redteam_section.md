## Task 01B

**Acquire RL experience that remains useful as the learner changes — VERDICT: needs fixes.**

Current raw formula: `S = 0.40(a1+a2+a3+a4)/4 + 0.60 aT.` See [01B §Reward](/workspace/task_design_v2_codex/01_data_distribution.md:259). All table payouts are estimates using F above; actual anchors and attainable outcomes are to verify.

### Audit of the task’s own saturation section and kill criteria

Its own ceiling incorrectly invites comparison with immediate perfection throughout learning, which may be unattainable from the supplied initialization. The warning that a textbook scheduler may be universal is credible; no evidence yet defeats it. Retain its merge-with-02A kill option, but test the combined nonstationary/prerequisite baseline and random-time curve before any saturation campaign. Larger graphs must alter decisions, not just increase a controller state vector. Source: [01B §Saturation resistance](/workspace/task_design_v2_codex/01_data_distribution.md:281); experiments: [01B §E2/E3](/workspace/task_design_v2_codex/01_data_distribution.md:322). The common protocol critique above also applies.

### Verdict justification

Fresh learner replay and complete acquisition charging are good design choices, but four deterministic snapshots do not measure the advertised curve area. Cost-aware nonstationary bandits with prerequisite replay could supply the full method without the proposed chain. Retain only after repairing the time estimand and demonstrating a substantial raw gap over that combined baseline; otherwise merge the useful acquisition component into 02A.

- **[P1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.15393)** [DoGE: Domain Reweighting with Generalization Estimation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.15393). Proxy-derived domain reweighting and inter-domain generalization; applicability to these stage-dependent tasks is an inference to test.
- **[P2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.06560)** [Hyperband: A Novel Bandit-Based Approach to Hyperparameter Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.06560). Successive-halving resource allocation; no published result is claimed on these proposed tasks.

A concrete dynamic-sampling RL baseline, not evidence that its default solves 01B.
- **[P17](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02629)** [Generalization in Adaptive Data Analysis and Holdout Reuse](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02629). Motivates controlling adaptive holdout reuse. A throttle alone is not a privacy or generalization proof; no complete v2 bank reconstruction is claimed.
