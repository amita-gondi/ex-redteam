**What the subfield has actually achieved.** The closest mature result located for the structured-model threat is Zolman and colleagues' **SINDy-RL (2025)**. Their human-engineered system repeatedly fits ensemble sparse dynamics, trains a policy in that surrogate, and collects additional real-system data. **UNMEASURED locally, author-reported:** across five control environments it improves sample efficiency over PPO by roughly 10–100 times; the experiments include fluid-flow control and gust mitigation. This supports the concern that compact identification can beat much larger neural models. It does not establish planner-resistant uncertainty: the paper uses ensemble variance diagnostically and describes uncertainty-guided exploration and risk penalties as further opportunities. Its learned-policy route also exceeds W10's permitted intervention. [SINDy-RL, full article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12663201/)

For the narrower fixed-MPC component, **Ensemble-SINDy (2021 preprint)** demonstrates noisy forced-Lorenz identification followed by stabilization of an unstable fixed point. **UNMEASURED locally, author-reported:** its control comparison includes models trained with 50 and 150 time-step observations and averages costs over 1,000 noise realizations. The paper also demonstrates disagreement-based acquisition, but explicitly leaves combining its active-learning and MPC strategies as a future extension. Even this unusually close literature result does not demonstrate the entire proposed attack. [Ensemble-SINDy, control results and discussion](https://arxiv.org/html/2111.10992v1)

On the neural planning side, **PETS (2018)** combines probabilistic ensembles with trajectory sampling. **UNMEASURED locally, author-reported:** on half-cheetah it matches model-free asymptotic performance using eight times fewer samples than SAC and 125 times fewer than PPO. These are benchmark-specific comparisons, not guarantees against arbitrary planner exploitation. Its human-written implementation is available through the inherited library; a residual structured-plus-neural hybrid still needs integration and control validation. [PETS](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.12114v2), [pinned MBRL-Lib README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/facebookresearch/mbrl-lib/4544e49f39462d72cba6337653abd4892bf45838/README.md)

A broader world-model frontier comparator is **TD-MPC2 (2024)**. **UNMEASURED locally, author-reported:** it improves over compared baselines across 104 continuous-control tasks and trains a single 317-million-parameter system on 80 tasks. Its reward model, terminal value and policy prior are central to its planner; W10 excludes those submitted components. Consequently this result establishes substantial human/system competence in learned-model control, but cannot serve as a legal W10 reference implementation. [TD-MPC2](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.16828v2)

The newer **Maran and colleagues result (2026)** gives sparse nonlinear-dynamics regret bounds with non-Gaussian noise and misspecification. The theorem's algorithm is explicitly computationally inefficient; its practical variant combines SINDy with SAC in a Dyna framework.
