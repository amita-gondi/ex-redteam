## Task 01A

**Reconstruct a training corpus whose apparent quality rankings reverse across stages — VERDICT: needs fixes.**

### Audit of the task’s own saturation section and kill criteria

The own-section warning about DoReMi, stage reversal and brute force is candid, but a reversal is compatible with a cheap contextual optimizer. Increasing source count is ineffective when the actionable dimension remains small. Retain the stated single-run/best-of-k kill rules, add the two missing outcome-only baselines, and require learner-specific physical anchors. Neither S=1 nor the proposed 32B confirmation establishes reserve for a 10T researcher. Source: [01A §Saturation resistance](/workspace/task_design_v2_codex/01_data_distribution.md:149); experiments: [01A §E2/E3](/workspace/task_design_v2_codex/01_data_distribution.md:190). The common protocol critique above also applies.

### Verdict justification

The data intervention is relevant and the source-ID reconstruction contract blocks straightforward transcript or weight substitution. The task has no demonstrated residual gap over transfer-aware reweighting and low-dimensional schedule search, and its average can obscure a failed target learner. Retain it as a design to fix, conditional on native-unit evidence that the joint landscape is both learnable and resistant to competent full-budget search; do not call it a saturation pass.
