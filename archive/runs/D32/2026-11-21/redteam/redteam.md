## Published recipe shortcut — web checked

- [BatchBALD: Efficient and Diverse Batch Acquisition for Deep Bayesian Active Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.08158) establishes joint, dependence-aware batch acquisition rather than independent uncertainty ranking. It does not establish complementary robot-label utility.
- [Active Learning for Convolutional Neural Networks: A Core-Set Approach](https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.00489) supplies representation-space coverage as an acquisition baseline. Coverage is relevant when action-support gaps, rather than unusual visual appearances, drive learning.
- [Adaptive Submodularity: A New Approach to Active Learning and Stochastic Optimization](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~dgolovin/papers/colt10.pdf) identifies conditions under which adaptive greedy policies are competitive. Those conditions must be checked; robot information complementarity does not automatically satisfy them.
