## Task 05B

**Accelerate a distributed training graph while preserving updates and resumable state — VERDICT: needs fixes.**

Current raw formula: `S = mean of four e_f for contract-valid complete updates; invalid-family work earns zero.` See [05B §Reward](/workspace/task_design_v2_codex/05_exact_systems.md:259). All table payouts are estimates using F above; actual anchors and attainable outcomes are to verify.

### Audit of the task’s own saturation section and kill criteria

The own-section known-kernel and tiny-gain kills are credible and should remain binding. The term “exact” spans bitwise paths and tolerated paths, so the legal constrained optimum is not specified yet. More shapes can be dispatched by a table; more interrupt points can be handled by a standard complete checkpoint. Keep this candidate only after the timing/validity path is bound and a legal stronger author control proves reserve. Source: [05B §Saturation resistance](/workspace/task_design_v2_codex/05_exact_systems.md:281); experiments: [05B §E2/E3](/workspace/task_design_v2_codex/05_exact_systems.md:322). The common protocol critique above also applies.

### Verdict justification

This has a clear accepted-profile fit and strong observable invariants. Its validity checks must certify the actual timed work, tolerated error must not accumulate silently, and resume must be a cold restoration with durable-state semantics. The design also needs a demonstrated legal speed frontier after standard kernels; strict exactness can otherwise make the task impossible or physically saturated rather than deep.

## Primary sources used for attack recipes

- **[P10](https://github.com/pytorch/torchtitan)** [TorchTitan official repository](https://github.com/pytorch/torchtitan). A real candidate inherited platform. No exactness-compatible speedup or particular upstream patch is claimed.
- **[P17](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02629)** [Generalization in Adaptive Data Analysis and Holdout Reuse](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02629). Motivates controlling adaptive holdout reuse. A throttle alone is not a privacy or generalization proof; no complete v2 bank reconstruction is claimed.
