## Task 05A

**Increase exact useful serving capacity across coupled sessions, adapters and model shapes — VERDICT: needs fixes.**

Current raw formula: `S = mean of six e_f; e_f is correct-on-time requested reference-token work/s divided by the family bound.` See [05A §Reward](/workspace/task_design_v2_codex/05_exact_systems.md:127). All table payouts are estimates using F above; actual anchors and attainable outcomes are to verify.

### Audit of the task’s own saturation section and kill criteria

The section recognizes hardware and engine-import risks, but neither is answered by varying adapter counts and graph widths. Fixed offered load can bound the utility below hardware capacity, while exact token requirements can reject ordinary numerically different optimizations. The 45-minute grade must also budget baseline, reference checking and repeated windows. Keep the shared hard kills, add demand saturation and full-engine controls, and pin the exactness equivalence class before collecting evidence. Source: [05A §Saturation resistance](/workspace/task_design_v2_codex/05_exact_systems.md:149); experiments: [05A §E2/E3](/workspace/task_design_v2_codex/05_exact_systems.md:190). The common protocol critique above also applies.

### Verdict justification

This remains a strong candidate for a systems task, conditional on a real capacity estimator and a precise service contract. All-offered-load denominators do not eliminate rational request shedding, warmup may hide unlimited specialization, and the attainable exact-output ceiling is unmeasured. Fix those issues and demonstrate raw headroom over the complete tuned-engine baseline before treating a low reward as difficult research.
