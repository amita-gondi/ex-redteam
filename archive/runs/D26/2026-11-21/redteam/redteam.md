## Saturation against the hypothetical 10T researcher / 12h

The central distinction is between predicting a changed geometry and cheaply measuring its consequences. A strong researcher can build a response surface over actual short continuations, learn when current sketches are unreliable and allocate further probes only at those boundaries. This need not reconstruct the author's transport explanation.
