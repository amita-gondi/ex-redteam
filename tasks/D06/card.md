# D06 — Spend an irrevocable budget on repairing or replacing training worlds

**Generator:** recovered lifecycle-control contract. **Stage × artifact:** research process × experiment plan. **Modality:** training environments and learning curves.

## Problem

An agent-training project has useful faulty worlds, expensive repair options and alternative support it can construct or acquire. Submit a resource controller choosing repairs, replacements and subsequent training under a trusted lifetime ledger. [Suite 11.9](/workspace/task_suites_claude.md:16112) makes these expenditures irreversible: discarded support cannot be recovered through an uncharged reset.

## Why known recipes plateau

Cost-sensitive bandits, repair-first heuristics and replacement by certified clean support are serious complete strategies. A possible residual is that repairing a rare prerequisite changes the value of later worlds, while replacing it loses unique support. That interaction must appear in real learning, not in an authored table of abstract utilities.

## Deterministic grader

Root executes each requested lifecycle action, debits measured or prevalidated costs, and trains the fixed learner using the resulting collection. U is broad and vulnerable-skill exact terminal capability at the frozen ledger endpoint, with separately disclosed strata. Unfinished campaigns stay in the denominator. Construction, failed repair and discarded-world costs are charged; F_D06 cannot reward cheapness without capability.

## Hidden-score design

Private environment families vary repairability and prerequisite overlap, within disclosed price/support ranges. Dev contains protected campaigns with matching diagnostic uncertainty. A controller sees only available histories and paid probes, not future campaign returns or private fault labels.

## Strongest attack + rough ESTIMATED reward

**ESTIMATED reward: F_D06(U_clean_replacement).** Assuming alternate clean worlds cover the same prerequisites, discard faulty worlds and run a contextual cost bandit over replacements. That legal strategy could remove repair's value. Trying to launder construction through cached manifests must fail trusted accounting, not be treated as an efficient discovery.
