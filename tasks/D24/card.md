## Problem

Submit a reusable controller choosing paid tests and selecting candidates across new pools; policies are not trained by the solver. ## Deterministic grader

Execute the controller against permitted diagnostic observations and freeze its selections. U is exact deployment value of selected candidates on a sealed finite outcome bank, with selection regret and pairwise ranking exported diagnostically. Candidate failures stay in their deployment denominators. Trusted rational/integer comparisons resolve finite-bank ties; no subjective model-ranking judge determines F_D24.
