## The research problem and its boundary

A post-training researcher inherits a reasoning/tool policy and a continuation whose first-attempt accuracy improved. The run history suggests that some previously reachable problems became less likely to succeed. The researcher must determine whether that apparent loss comes from exposure imbalance, shortened completions, noisy support measurement, or interference between updates, then improve the deployed policy under the same inference budget. The requested outcome is higher first-attempt success, retained finite-budget coverage, and better repeated execution reliability on fresh tasks.

This is useful in an AI lab because a policy used to generate experiments or execute tool transactions must remain useful across problems after optimization. A training change that makes common operations dependable while erasing a rare transaction type can make the next research loop worse. The subject's successful actions are checked against executable semantics. Reports, plausible reasoning, entropy statistics, and the number of experiments run do not earn reward.

The task concerns **across-problem retention**, not proof of new reasoning modes or algorithmic diversity. A learned deterministic correct policy is a legitimate reliability improvement. The evaluator never rewards different wording or sampled paths. “Support” means probability of success within the frozen repeated-attempt budget; finite failures cannot prove mathematical probability zero.

Published evidence does not establish that every RL run loses support. [Does Reinforcement Learning Really Incentivize Reasoning Capacity in LLMs Beyond the Base Model?](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/537d5aa768c2d534016a4d06f87bc8fb-Paper-Conference.pdf) motivates measuring both sampling efficiency and large-sample coverage. [Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards Implicitly Incentivizes Correct Reasoning in Base LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.14245) reports contrary boundary-expansion evidence. These are precedents for a measurement question, not T06 results or proof of its causal chain. The inherited effect must be reproduced before task construction proceeds.

No souping, parameter averaging, task-vector arithmetic, specialist consolidation or model merging is included. The research artifact is a continuation-training algorithm applied to one authorized inherited policy. The original earlier policy can supply lawful replay/logit targets; it is never combined with the submitted parameters by interpolation.
