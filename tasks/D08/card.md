## Problem

Historical row attribution is uncertain because donor versions and event arrivals were joined imperfectly. Submit an attribution and replay-routing policy over declared donor evidence, as in [Suite 3.8](/workspace/task_suites_claude.md:3576) and Suite 8.12. Transfer uses examples and learning only.
