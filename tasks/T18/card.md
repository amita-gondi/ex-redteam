## Problem, motivation and scientific boundary

A post-training researcher inherits a working policy-gradient run that uses a separately operated reward service. Reward-unit conversion, token alignment and behavior log-probability reconstruction have already been repaired. Nevertheless, bad reward labels cluster in some groups and correlate with likelihood features. As the actor moves over several updates, some of those same trajectories acquire large genuine importance weights. Discarding all tails removes both erroneous rewards and scarce correct reasoning. The task is to learn more clean, executable capability from the same rollout, audit and training resources.

This is an optimizer-research problem: decide which evidence distinguishes reward corruption from valuable rare outcomes, construct a useful objective, and allocate limited work between additional groups, reuse and a critic. It models a lab optimizer team consuming telemetry from a reward pipeline whose text and checker implementation are owned by another service. The restricted observation boundary is part of that organizational setting; it cannot be a request to ignore a checker that is actually readable. Transfer to unrestricted end-to-end RL training is an empirical question, not an asserted result.

The final outcome is the subject actor’s success on clean private tasks. No score rewards outlier detection, posterior fit, small KL, low gradient kurtosis, stable training, a claimed insight or a written report. There is no model merging. The outer researcher may use known algorithms and replace its entire optimizer controller within the published interface.

The closest accepted shape is **pretraining_latest**: a protected trainer owns model transitions, while the researcher conducts multi-hour learning experiments and submits an intervention that is re-executed. Unlike **inference_optimization**, T18 does not pay throughput or compare against a cold serving baseline. Unlike **agentic_search_inference_efficiency**, it needs neither a hosted answering model nor a latency bonus. The common requirement is independently checked useful model work. Reserving accelerators for a prose diagnosis would fail A6/E13.

The supplied Sol/Astra and Opus weakness reports motivate testing sustained experiment selection, scientific verification and novel post-training decisions. They also warn that strong systems recipes can saturate quickly. Their anecdotal early stopping, benchmark scores and model self-reports are not evidence that T18 resists a stronger researcher. ### Distinct likelihoods

For a response with valid generated-token set V, the trainer computes, in declared accumulation precision,

- `ell_ref = mean_{t in V}(log pi_behavior(a_t|h_t) - log pi_reference(a_t|h_t))`;
- `d_t(theta) = log pi_theta(a_t|h_t) - log pi_behavior(a_t|h_t)`;
- `rho_seq(theta) = exp(mean_{t in V} d_t(theta))`, and `rho_token,t = exp(d_t(theta))`.

These are ESTIMATED design definitions, consistent with the distinct quantities in `T18_source.txt`. `pi_reference` is the earlier authenticated actor; `pi_behavior` is the exact snapshot that sampled this response; `pi_theta` is the current learner. On a newly sampled on-policy group, the learner/behavior ratios start at unity. They develop tails through actual policy movement and reuse. `ell_ref` may already differ from zero. Clipping `ell_ref` inside a corruption service does not change stored log probabilities or the true importance ratios. Sequence length normalization and a full sequence probability ratio are different quantities and must never share an ambiguous `ratio` column.

The trainer, not a submitted callback, constructs masks, token counts, probabilities and snapshot hashes. V includes sampled response tokens and a sampled EOS, excludes prompt/padding tokens, and respects the frozen response truncation boundary. Store both token and sequence telemetry and the snapshot pair used for each update. Reject empty V. Test identity at sampling, replay after an actual update, masking and length changes against an independent small exact implementation. A synthetic large ratio assigned at sampling would invalidate the intended mechanism.

## Known methods, combined floor and constructive reference

The checked primary sources establish mechanisms, not T18 outcomes:

- [Noise-corrected GRPO: From Noisy Rewards to Unbiased Gradients](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.18924) estimates reward-flip probabilities and corrects group-based learning signals. T18 cannot claim novelty merely from binary reward noise. Its conditional/group-dependent extension belongs in the baseline.
- [Dual-Channel Robust Group-Relative Policy Optimization via Advantage and Sequence-Weight Estimation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36944) treats reward-derived contrasts and likelihood weights through separate robust channels. Supply a faithful tested port as a baseline before asking whether their dependence matters. Its published perturbation setting does not validate T18’s post-repair joint law.
