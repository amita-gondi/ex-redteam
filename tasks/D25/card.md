# D25 — Invest in reusable training support across learner campaigns

**Generator:** persistent-investment recovery. **Stage × artifact:** research process × experiment plan. **Modality:** support inventories and learning campaigns.

## Problem

A training organization repeatedly pays to rebuild diagnostic or environment support that could have been reused, while some old support becomes obsolete. Submit an investment/use controller over a persistent support inventory with trusted depreciation semantics. [Suite 11.16](/workspace/task_suites_claude.md:16888) charges once-only construction across campaigns under a shared lifetime budget.

## Why known recipes plateau

Break-even analysis, dynamic programming, cost bandits, reusable caches and myopic learner-progress allocation form a strong baseline. A genuine residual requires support investments to alter later learnability in ways revealed only after earlier campaigns. If all future values are a fixed table, this collapses to ordinary capital-budget planning.

## Deterministic grader

Root executes investment and support-use actions, trains campaign learners and debits construction, maintenance, obsolete work and evaluation from the persistent ledger. U is exact final capability averaged across all scheduled campaigns and vulnerable skill strata. Failed campaigns remain scored; unused investment receives no intrinsic credit. F_D25 values learning produced by the entire lifecycle, not reusable artifact count.

## Hidden-score design

Fresh campaign mechanisms change which support generalizes and how observed drift affects reuse. Dev matches the disclosed drift/depreciation law and includes protected campaign-composition proxies. Future returns are not visible; inventory handles cannot survive outside their allowed lifecycle or silently reset costs.

## Strongest attack + rough ESTIMATED reward

**ESTIMATED reward: F_D25(U_cost_bandit).** Assuming support usefulness is predictable from observed reuse and decay, a contextual cost bandit with full-budget planning may attain reference utility. Compare build-all-common-support and never-invest as complete controls. Reusing an uncharged prebuilt asset is an accounting exploit, not a scientific result.

## Saturation note vs 10T / 12h

A stronger researcher can plan investments globally and fit the decay model. Reserve requires actual support×learner×future-campaign interactions after those controls; adding campaigns cannot create it by itself. Index hard gates include all shared preparation and selection costs, not only final-campaign compute.

## Cheapest decisive check

**ESTIMATED: 22 GPU-h** including repeated learner campaigns and final tests. Cross reusable versus campaign-specific support with stable versus changing prerequisites, comparing optimal available-cost controls. Stop if break-even planning explains the entire advantage.
