## The problem and its research value

A researcher maintaining a constrained adaptation service can afford to modify only a small part of a deployed model. A selected weight can be useful for the next training stage yet expensive to gather, differentiate or repack. A previously selected weight can be needed again after a domain returns. Sparse Adam state saves little time if the service still materializes dense weight gradients, and an early support allocation can consume the entire lifetime allowance before later domains arrive. T09 asks an agent to improve held-out learned capability and synchronized, end-to-end training time on the same legal trajectory. The result is a support policy and executable training implementation, reconstructed and checked by a separate grader.

The authentic role is an adaptation researcher working with an ML systems engineer on an inherited dense-backed sparse trainer. The inherited project must contain actual baseline runs, failed packing experiments, coordinate traces and profiler captures. These histories must be produced by executing the packaged starter; fabricated logs and planted coordinate-index bugs cannot supply research depth. Coordinate identity, moment bookkeeping, weight-decay scope, sorting, ordinary fusion and shape warmup are corrected **before** the research start. The starting implementation may still compute dense gradients and pack inefficiently, but it must already implement the declared mathematics correctly.

The intended scientific uncertainty is response redundancy: whether interchangeable useful coordinates can be selected and reused in layouts that permit a cheaper selected-gradient dependency graph. Merely counting trainable parameters, observing low loss on training data, or shrinking optimizer storage does not answer that question. The static-superset attack is especially serious because removing a coordinate never refunds lifetime capacity. If a static superset with a standard block schedule wins, that is a successful legal solution and a failure of T09’s research premise.

This has the accepted shape of `pretraining_latest` through genuine weight updates and private downstream evaluation, and of `inference_optimization` through exact operator validation and external timing. As in `agentic_search`, changing an upstream policy can change downstream work and quality together. Unlike a report-only task, T09’s claimed speed is measured on executed kernels and the resulting learned state. These comparisons come from GDM Round 2; they do not confer acceptance. No souping, model merging, checkpoint averaging or specialist fusion is part of T09.

### Exact support and displacement law

This paragraph freezes the **proposed** law rather than delegating it to an implementer. The eligible set E consists of scalar coordinates of attention projection and MLP projection weight matrices in every transformer layer. Embeddings, output head, normalization parameters, biases, rotary constants and other buffers are frozen. The manifest enumerates actual names, shapes and canonical row-major offsets; tied storage is deduplicated by canonical parameter identity before N=|E| is computed. No model parameter count is invented here.

Adopt Suite 13.7’s source-file **ESTIMATED** active fraction and lifetime ratio: `K=floor(0.001*N)` and `L=2*K`. At every optimizer step `|S_t|<=K`; `U_t=union_{s<=t} S_s` must satisfy `|U_t|<=L`. Selecting a coordinate counts toward the union even when its gradient, learning rate or resulting delta is zero. All write channels, including decay, resets and serialization, use this same accounting. A block claims every scalar in its block; padding and duplicate IDs cannot hide work or enlarge capacity. Selecting fewer than K is legal, with the full prescribed training trajectory still required.

**Eviction removes update eligibility, not history.** Its cumulative delta remains part of the model’s forward pass. Its moment state remains indexed by canonical coordinate identity. Neither zeroing a delta nor renaming a packed slot refunds union capacity. Returning coordinates recover their own moments; a slot cannot inherit another coordinate’s moments. Resetting evicted weights to base would be an additional write, and is not in this intervention. At the end, every coordinate outside U is bitwise equal to its initial stored value; inside U the trusted reconstruction contains precisely the accepted cumulative deltas. A static superset of size L, cycling active subsets of size K, is explicitly legal.

The canonical recurrence is sparse AdamW with global-step bias correction. For a selected coordinate i at step t, let p be its last active step and initialize unseen moments to zero with p=0. Define `m_t=beta1^(t-p)*m_p+(1-beta1)*g_t`, `v_t=beta2^(t-p)*v_p+(1-beta2)*g_t^2`; then `mhat=m_t/(1-beta1^t)`, `vhat=v_t/(1-beta2^t)` and `w_t=(1-eta_t*lambda)*w_{t-1}-eta_t*mhat/(sqrt(vhat)+epsilon)`. Inactive weights do not decay; their moments are represented lazily with their last-active timestamps. This is an explicit zero-gradient moment-decay convention, not a claim that every published block optimizer uses it. Proposed constants are **ESTIMATED** `beta1=0.9`, `beta2=0.999`, `epsilon=1e-8`, `lambda=0.01`. For total steps T define `W=ceil(0.05*T)`; `eta_t=2e-5*t/W` for `1<=t<=W`, and `eta_t=1e-5*(1+cos(pi*(t-W)/(T-W)))` thereafter. The trusted schedule is identical for all methods. These fixed choices must survive feasibility checks before freeze; changing them creates a new version and restarts anchors.

There are **ESTIMATED 256 update steps**, each consuming **ESTIMATED 4,096 nonpadding training tokens**, for **ESTIMATED 1,048,576 tokens per seed**. The public shape inventory covers padded sequence buckets **ESTIMATED 256, 512 and 1,024 tokens**, with microbatch/accumulation identities fixed in the manifest. The stream has **ESTIMATED four equal stages**, including a returning-domain stage. Stage semantics and ordinary training examples are visible as they arrive; later stages are unavailable before their release step. Support can be reconsidered at every step. There is no forced churn, minimum refresh count or novelty bonus. A controller that retains the same support is legal. Earlier-stage diagnostics and support changes are included in training time.

The training objective is fixed teacher-forced next-token cross-entropy, summed over the prescribed nonpadding prediction targets and divided by their count at each effective update. The exposure ledger defines token IDs, target masks, order, microbatch accumulation order and stage release; padding contributes neither loss nor exposure credit. Dropout is disabled. There is no policy-gradient reward, generated-label loop or candidate-written loss. This chooses the physical-efficiency witness’s adaptation setting; the catalog’s other sparse-RL variants are not silently claimed validated by this supervised instance. A solver can inspect lawful diagnostic copies but cannot change the trusted target masks or exposures.

### Submission ABI and actual mutation ownership

Submit `policy.py`, textual kernel sources under `kernels/`, and `config.json`. The policy is an isolated process given step ID, remaining lifetime capacity, stage-visible training statistics and copies of legal model/gradient diagnostics. Its only responses are a canonical support ID list, a layout permutation and a choice among predeclared compiled operators. It cannot return weights, moment tensors, arbitrary gradients, loss values or generated training labels. Probe requests that require extra model work are metered and timed. No future-stage or evaluation record is an input to this process.

The kernel worker receives only training operands and disposable copies of model/state buffers. It emits selected gradients, losses, cumulative selected deltas and moment/timestamp state with exact step and support versions. It holds no CUDA IPC handle or shared writable storage belonging to the canonical trainer or evaluator. **Only trainer-owned scatter** mutates canonical state. Compiled code never loads in the truth-owning Python process or CUDA context. Worker storage aliases are harmless to canonical state only if this separation actually works; that claim needs live driver/IPC tests.

Compilation accepts source and public shapes, never hidden operands, seeds or checkpoint deltas. The permitted kernel grammar is numerical tensor code: fixed-shape loads/stores, bounded loops, arithmetic/reductions and dispatch over declared operators. No host callbacks, file reads, dynamic import, device printf, network, inline external binary, mutable device-global table or arbitrary state-return operation is admitted. The policy may run general bounded Python in its own sandbox; it has no canonical mutation capability. This permits algorithmic support selection while preventing a controller from becoming a weight injector. A legitimate novel kernel must pass the same ABI. If this grammar cannot express a credible selected-gradient improvement, revise the claim before build rather than opening an undocumented arbitrary-code channel.

All accepted transitions are independently replayed from trainer-owned operands. The trusted trainer checks **every step**, every selected scalar gradient, moment and cumulative delta, scalar loss, and every support/version/timestamp. Frozen storage can only be changed by canonical scatter, and the trainer logs all such writes; full frozen hashes at initialization, refresh, final save and cold restore detect implementation mistakes without being the sole every-write defense. Evaluation constructs weights from immutable base plus the accepted canonical delta ledger; it never loads a worker-supplied model, optimizer checkpoint or tokenizer. The trusted replay is for mathematical validity only and never supplies the speed denominator.

## Baselines, reference and attainable anchors

The independent review identified the following published routes. These references support mechanisms, not scores, compatibility or guarantees on T09:

- [BAdam: A Memory Efficient Full Parameter Optimization Method for Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.02827) uses block-coordinate descent with Adam. It motivates cyclic/importance-based block controls; unrestricted full-parameter cycling would exceed T09’s lifetime union.
- [BlockLLM: Memory-Efficient Adaptation of LLMs by Selecting and Optimizing the Right Coordinate Blocks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.17296) selects and optimizes coordinate blocks. Block choice is already a known method and must not be credited as a novel insight by itself.
- [GaLore: Memory-Efficient LLM Training by Gradient Low-Rank Projection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.03507) reduces optimizer-state memory through gradient projection. A broad projected weight update is not coordinate sparse, so a faithful unrestricted implementation is a resource-matched external comparator; any coordinate-restricted adaptation must be described separately.
- [LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685) trains added low-rank factors with frozen base weights. It is a useful resource-matched alternative, but its effective dense delta and extra factors do not automatically satisfy this fixed-coordinate, fixed-architecture contract.

Run resource-matched dense, GaLore and LoRA comparators to challenge whether T09’s intervention is useful at all. They cannot establish a feasible T09 ceiling if they violate K/L or change architecture. Also run dense-backed execution of *legal identical sparse updates*, which is feasible and eligible as an anchor. A low-rank factorization restricted to a selected coordinate block is legal only when every effective changed coordinate and all deployed state are accounted for; it still obeys the fixed optimizer mathematics. Standard dense/LoRA success can undermine the practical motivation even without winning the constrained score.
