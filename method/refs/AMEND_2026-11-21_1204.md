
BINDING AMENDMENT (project lead, 12:04 PM PT Nov 21 2026) — applies to EVERY card, including ones already written
Concern: attack estimates measured the attacker against our own idea of what could be done, not against what SOTA agents have actually been shown to do. Therefore:
(1) Each card's strongest-attack estimate must cite DEMONSTRATED results: a published or measured agent/model result on that capability, or the closest comparable one (e.g., RE-Bench, METR time-horizon results, MLE-bench, PaperBench / PaperBench Science, KernelBench / KernelAgent, AlphaEvolve, AI Scientist-v2, autoresearch, PostTrainBench, frontier model system-card ML-R&D evals, plus domain-specific SOTA papers/leaderboards for the card's subfield). For each: what was achieved, by which model and scaffold, in what time and compute, and how far that is from what this card's attack needs (the gap). Fetch everything you cite; never fabricate; mark anything unverified. /workspace/creative_v4_screen_audit/frontier_reference.md has a starting list (fetched) — you may use it, but you must open (fetch) any source you cite and tie it to THIS card's capability specifically.
(2) Separate explicitly: KNOWN-RECIPE (a human-built recipe exists in the literature) vs AGENT-DEMONSTRATED (a frontier agent has been shown to find, integrate and validate it unaided within a comparable budget). attack_basis = DEMONSTRATED-AGENT only for the latter; LITERATURE-ONLY when only a human recipe exists; ASSUMED when neither. Only DEMONSTRATED-AGENT justifies a high attack score. Where only a known recipe exists, score the attack accordingly (ESTIMATED, task-specific reasoning: what an agent must discover/integrate/validate in 12 h beyond what has been demonstrated) and say explicitly that the verdict rests on assumption. Do not reflexively lower everything either: if agents have demonstrated the capability (e.g., autoresearch-style short-loop HPO, kernel speedups, post-training gap closure), say so and score high.
(3) Research the subfield's actual SOTA: best known human or system result, with source and year. Anchor the card's north star / reference solution (what R≈1 or the reference R corresponds to) to it, not to an invented one.
(4) Produce an ANCHORED estimate and ANCHORED verdict (this is the headline result).
